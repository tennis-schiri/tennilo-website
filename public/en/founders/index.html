01 / THE PROJECT

What we are raising funds for

Support would fund the entire TENNILO product family. Score, Tournament, Umpire and ClubDisplay are equal parts of the campaign. Funds will be allocated in a balanced way according to development needs, testing and shared infrastructure, rather than a rigid allocation. No equity or investment returns are offered.

01 / DEVELOPMENT

TENNILO Score

A scorekeeping app for Apple Watch and iPhone: intuitive scoring, testing, reliability and release preparation.

02 / DEVELOPMENT

TENNILO Tournament

Digital tools for clubs and organizers: tournament management, workflows and results.

03 / CONCEPT

TENNILO Umpire

Digital support for umpires and officials; initially concept and feasibility.

04 / PILOT PHASE

TENNILO ClubDisplay

BASIC: automatic club information screens. PLUS: interactive terminals with TENNILO SmartDisplay, partner board and approved booking integration. Pilot at Neuenhagener Tennisclub; integrations subject to approval.

06 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is registration binding?

No. It is only a non-binding expression of interest. No payment or contract is created.

Can I buy a package now?

No. Once the campaign is approved, packages and free contributions will be available exclusively through Startnext. TENNILO does not accept payments on its own website.

Do I have to support Founders to join the beta?

No. The planned Score beta is separate. An Apple Watch is required; there is no fixed participant limit. The planned free app at market readiness for beta testers is independent of Founders.

Do I receive company shares?

No. This is a planned crowdfunding initiative on Startnext, not an investment with profit participation.

How can I unsubscribe?

Use the unsubscribe function or email founders@tennilo.de.
