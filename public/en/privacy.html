Privacy policy

How personal data is processed when visiting this TENNILO information website (last updated: November 2026).

This is an English translation of our German privacy policy.

Michael Wiedeck (Tennis-Schiri), Hauptstraße 129, 15366 Neuenhagen bei Berlin, Germany.

2. Website access and server logs

When you access the website, technically necessary information is processed. This may include your device's IP address, date and time, requested pages and files, data volume transferred, browser information, referrer and web server status messages.

The data is processed to deliver the website, maintain technical stability, and detect and prevent attacks. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in operating this website securely).

Server logs are retained only as long as necessary for these technical and security purposes. Longer retention may be required to investigate specific security incidents or comply with legal obligations. The exact deletion period depends on the hosting provider's server settings.

3. Hosting

The website uses hosting infrastructure provided by ALL-INKL.COM – Neue Medien Münnich, owner René Münnich, Hauptstraße 68, 02742 Friedersdorf, Germany. Access data described in Section 2 may be processed on the hosting provider's servers. Where required, processing is governed by a data processing agreement under Article 28 GDPR.

4. Cookies, analytics and third-party content

The public information website does not use analytics or advertising trackers, and it does not embed content from external platforms. The protected Founders administration area uses a technically necessary session cookie. If you manually switch between light and dark modes, only your color preference is stored locally in your browser (local storage). Without a manual choice, your system preference applies. This color preference is not transmitted to us and is not used for tracking. Fonts, stylesheets and images are served by this website. Therefore, no consent banner is currently provided for the features described here.

5. Contact by email or telephone

When you contact us, we process the contact details you provide and the content of your message in order to respond. Depending on the reason for contacting us, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual or contractual communication) or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in handling enquiries). Messages are deleted when they are no longer needed, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

6. TENNILO Founders waitlist

When you voluntarily sign up, we process your email address, an optional interest-group selection and the topics you choose (beta tests, Founders offers, crowdfunding) to send you relevant information. The legal basis is your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. Registration is free and does not require a purchase.

We use a double opt-in process to confirm registration. We record the registration and confirmation times and the version of the consent text. Unconfirmed registrations are deleted after 14 days. Confirmed entries are retained until you withdraw consent or the purpose no longer applies. You can unsubscribe at any time using the online unsubscribe function, the link in our emails, or by writing to founders@tennilo.de. When you unsubscribe, your waitlist entry is deleted.

The data is stored in a private area of our German hosting infrastructure and processed for email confirmations and updates. A notification is sent internally to founders@tennilo.de after a registration is confirmed. For information emails, we log the chosen interest topic, sending status and delivery attempt. Successful handoff to a mail server does not necessarily mean the email was delivered. Signing up for the waitlist alone does not involve transferring your data to crowdfunding platforms. To prevent automated registrations, pseudonymized technical request data is processed temporarily, solely for abuse prevention.

7. General TENNILO newsletter and email preferences

Separately from the Founders waitlist, you can subscribe to the general TENNILO newsletter for news about features, tournament formats and development. The legal basis is your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. The newsletter is free. We currently plan approximately one email every four to six weeks, with occasional additional messages for significant news.

To register, we process your email address and send a confirmation email from news@tennilo.de. Your newsletter subscription becomes active only after clicking the confirmation link. We store your preferences, consent time and consent version in a private data store. Unconfirmed newsletter requests are deleted after 14 days. Links for managing email topics remain valid for one hour.

Contact information is managed in a shared contact database, with separate consents for the general newsletter, beta tests, Founders offers and crowdfunding. Joining the Founders waitlist does not automatically subscribe you to the general newsletter. You can change your choices or delete them entirely using the email preference management page. You can unsubscribe from general newsletters separately without cancelling other chosen topics.

To prevent automated form submissions, pseudonymized identifiers derived from requests and email addresses are processed temporarily and deleted within 24 hours. Our mailing system records which emails were passed to the mail server or not sent. We do not track email opens or clicks.

8. Sharing data and transfers to third countries

We disclose personal data only where necessary for operating the website, responding to enquiries, or complying with legal obligations. The functions described here do not send analytics or advertising data to third parties. The website does not plan to actively transfer visitor data to services outside the European Economic Area.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right of access (Article 15 GDPR), rectification (Article 16), erasure (Article 17), restriction of processing (Article 18), data portability (Article 20) and, for processing based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, the right to object (Article 21). You may exercise these rights using the contact details above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Article 77 GDPR), including the State Commissioner for Data Protection and the Right of Access to Files in Brandenburg (Germany).

10. Encryption and changes

The live website is delivered over encrypted HTTPS connections. This privacy policy will be updated if technical features, service providers or applicable legal requirements change.
