For tennis players

Score. Focus on the match.

TENNILO Score is being developed for intuitive point tracking on Apple Watch, supported by the iPhone. The interactive scorer illustrates the concept – it is not the finished app.

01

Direct input

Add points on Apple Watch with as few steps as possible.

02

Clear match overview

Keep points, games, sets and tiebreaks easy to follow.

03

Tennis rules

Take service order, changeovers and different scoring systems into account.

04

Doubles and mixed

Support the particular demands of doubles and mixed tennis in development.

PRODUCT INFORMATION

What is planned?

The app is not yet released. The prototypes and beta programme are being developed and validated in real tennis situations.

Platforms

Designed for Apple Watch and iPhone. Apple Watch is required for the announced on-court test.

Match formats

Singles and doubles, with tiebreaks, service tracking and different scoring systems considered during development.

Beta participation

Planned for December 27, 2026 at a ranking tournament in Bad Saarow. Beta participants are intended to receive the released app free of charge.

No fixed tester limit and currently no separate beta signup on this website. Dates and functions are planning assumptions.
