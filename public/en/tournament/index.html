TENNILO TOURNAMENT / FOR CLUBS AND ORGANISERS

In development · release date not confirmed

Organise tennis events with clarity.

TENNILO Tournament is being developed to support tennis events, from informal club formats to structured match schedules. The capabilities below represent the intended product direction.

Planned features →Development status →

FEATURES

Planned capabilities

These features are goals for the product. Individual modules are not yet offered as a publicly available application.

Event formats

Club events, social mixed formats, singles and doubles are among the intended uses.

Players and teams

Collect participants, organise doubles pairings and keep entry lists clear.

Draws and matchups

Create and adjust tennis pairings in a transparent, understandable way.

Scheduling and courts

Bring matches, available courts and time slots into a single plan.

Results

Record results, follow event progress and make match outcomes understandable.

Presentation

Eventually share schedules, pairings and results with participants or digital club displays.

WORKFLOW

An example workflow

This is a product concept, not a claim that automated workflows are already available.

01

Prepare the event

Select a format and basic settings, then add participants.

02

Plan the matches

Organise pairings and assign matches to courts and times.

03

Present results

Record results and keep the event overview up to date.

TARGET GROUPS

Who is Tournament for?

Club organisers

Reduce administrative coordination for club competitions and events.

Event directors

A central workspace for player lists, schedules and results.

Participants

Clear information about matches, start times and results.

PRODUCT STATUS

Current status and limitations

Tournament remains in development. The features described here are planning goals. There is no committed launch date, no confirmed integration with third-party competition platforms and no public purchase flow.
