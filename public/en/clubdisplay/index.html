ClubDisplay in everyday use

Operation & information mode

Six touch tiles, German/English, fixed navigation, time and weather. Automatic rotation with pause, manual controls and return after inactivity.

Smart displays

Welcome messages for home fixtures and events, automatic weekend preview, tournament countdown from 100 days. Context-sensitive slide prioritisation.

Fixtures & events

Home/away, team, league, date, time, result, match status, confirmed courts and venues. Multi-day events with date range and location; adaptive pagination.

Courts & booking

Outdoor and indoor occupancy with time windows and status. PLUS: compatible external booking terminal, e.g. eBuSy after approval. Personal occupancy data only with appropriate authorisation; demo bookings are simulated.

Weather & radar

Current conditions, wind, rain probability, hourly and multi-day forecasts, sunrise/sunset and regional radar. Source and outage indicators.

News & newsletter

Up to three latest website posts with internal article reader. Approved original PDFs with page and zoom controls and archive (NTC pilot).

Sponsors & dining

Logo grid, weekly offers, regular and special opening hours, reservation information and website QR code.

Playing partners & communities

Four anonymous requests per page with individual QR codes; smartphone view. Separately approved youth and adult WhatsApp QR codes (NTC pilot).

Club branding

Club logo, accent colours, welcome message, individual display address and configurable screen profiles.

Central club administration

Club access & branding

Separate protected club login restricted to the club’s own data. Logo upload, colour, welcome message. Latest 100 audit entries.

Editorial content & media

Create, edit and delete news, events, sponsors and dining entries. Title, text, image, validity dates, draft/approval. Target all or selected paired devices.

Display profiles & pairing

Multiple profiles with individual mode, modules, sequence, duration per module, language, welcome and activation. Preview, issue, regenerate and revoke device keys.

Data sources

Configure club-specific data sources and test approved feeds. Booking terminal requires compatible provider permission and PLUS configuration.

Dining & sponsors

Manage regular and special opening hours, weekly offers, notices and images/logos centrally; publish according to enabled modules.

Newsletter & WhatsApp · pilot

NTC: upload PDFs by issue month, add or replace originals, archive, preview, protected download, explicit public approval or withdrawal. Upload, enable, hide and replace QR images individually.

Partner board management

Activation, notification address, dashboard counters, pending approvals, active requests, expiry, renewal, deletion and contact mediation. Outgoing email queue and pending status mail retries.

TENNILO operator administration

Manage clubs, licence type, modules, club accounts, contract details, display quotas and billing. Billing CSV export and SQLite backup download.

Security & permissions

Club-scoped access checks, protected sessions, CSRF protection, individually revocable device keys and protected SQLite/media storage. Admin PIN for app fullscreen exit; OS kiosk mode also required.

Playing partner board · complete workflow

1 · QR code & form

Anonymous overview or direct request. Game type, skill, optional LK rating, multiple days/times, own and preferred age bands and optional gender. Email required, phone optional.

2 · Verification & approval

Confirm email. Club receives 'View request', checks membership, identity and youth safeguards and decides in a protected detail view. 14-day publication, renewal with review, withdrawal and status emails.

3 · Protected mediation

No contact details in public requests. Review interest separately; adult interested players’ contact details may be shared with the requester after consent and approval. Protected mediation for minors, guardian submission under 16.

Requirements & setup

Hardware

  • Recommended: 24–43 inches, Full HD (1920 × 1080), landscape.
  • Mini PC or existing computer, Windows 11 recommended; current browser.
  • Touchscreen for interaction; ordinary monitor for display-only use.

Operation

  • Stable internet, power and suitable kiosk/autostart setup.
  • Club logo, colours, contact and approved data sources.
  • Protected club administration for content and display profiles after activation; separate TENNILO operator access.

Integrations & privacy

  • nuLiga/WordPress subject to compatibility checks; no official partnership implied.
  • Booking-system licence and terminal/API approval from provider and club.
  • Personal booking data only with lawful display and access safeguards.

Hosting & technical infrastructure

Club-provided hosting · standard

ClubDisplay is preferably installed on the club's own webspace, using its domain or subdomain with HTTPS. The club pays its hosting costs. Setup follows a technical compatibility check; responsibilities for updates, backups and operation are agreed in the quotation.

Technical web hosting requirements

Supported PHP 8.2+ (8.3+ recommended), PDO SQLite, GD for media, cURL, DOM/XML, SimpleXML, JSON. Apache .htaccess protection or agreed equivalent. HTTPS, outbound HTTPS, writable protected storage/cache, FTPS/SFTP. No standard self-installation package.

Database, storage & email

SQLite stores profiles, media, requests, devices and approvals; no separate database server. Newsletter up to 10 MB per PDF; PHP upload_max_filesize at least 10 MB and post_max_size at least 11 MB. Mail infrastructure, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, maintenance for status mail/expiry and sufficient backup storage.

Sources & interfaces

nuLiga public fixtures (not a guaranteed API), WordPress REST/RSS, club calendar/iCalendar, Bright Sky/DWD and radar/map services subject to availability. Structural changes may require adjustments.

Booking system & kiosk

eBuSy in NTC pilot; terminal/API permission and licence from provider/club. PIN and binding booking remain with eBuSy. Validate actual booking, access safeguards and sustained operation on target device.

Privacy & responsibility

Review public QR/PDF approvals, image rights, retention, permissions and mail delivery. No blanket GDPR guarantee. Agree technical and organisational measures, contract and support processes for each installation.
